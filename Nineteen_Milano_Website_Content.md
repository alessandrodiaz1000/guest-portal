# Milan City Guide

## Duomo di Milano

The iconic Gothic cathedral and symbol of Milan.

**Highlights**
- One of the most famous landmarks in Italy.
- Rooftop terraces offer breathtaking panoramic city views.

---

## Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II

Elegant historic shopping gallery.

**Highlights**
- Luxury boutiques.
- Historic architecture.
- Cafés and restaurants.
- Located next to the Duomo.

---

## Teatro alla Scala

One of the world's most famous opera houses.

**Highlights**
- Internationally renowned performances.
- Museum open to visitors even without a show ticket.

---

## Castello Sforzesco

Historic castle in central Milan.

**Highlights**
- Museums.
- Art exhibitions.
- Historic courtyards.
- Important Renaissance landmark.

---

## Parco Sempione

Largest park in Milan’s city center.

**Highlights**
- Located behind Castello Sforzesco.
- Perfect for walks, relaxation, and outdoor activities.

---

## Brera District

Milan’s artistic neighborhood.

**Highlights**
- Art galleries.
- Cobblestone streets.
- Brera Academy.
- Charming cafés and cultural atmosphere.

---

## Navigli

Historic canal district.

**Highlights**
- Picturesque canals.
- Restaurants and bars.
- Vibrant nightlife.
- Popular aperitivo destination.

---

## Museo del Novecento

Museum dedicated to modern Italian art.

**Highlights**
- Extensive collection of 20th-century Italian art.
- Unique views overlooking the Duomo.

---

## CityLife

Modern architectural district.

**Highlights**
- Contemporary skyscrapers.
- Shopping district.
- Public park.
- Modern urban design and architecture.
